\subsection{Verdicts of all hypotheses}
Table~\ref{tab:verdicts} lists every hypothesis that was specified before its data were analyzed, with the verdict produced by code from its criterion. The criteria are in the decision logs released with the code. ``Not evaluable'' means that a precondition of the criterion failed: in the v1 multi-objective study, the conditioning gate failed and the budgets differed; in v3, the calibration set the weight of the flood prior to zero, and no training run of the main matrix passed the learning gate. Experiment E was specified before any of its jobs ran, but after the v3 baseline ceiling had been seen, so its criteria are exploratory.

\subsection{Changes to the pre-specified plan}
Table~\ref{tab:changes} lists the changes made to the v3 plan after it was committed, and when each was decided relative to the data. Ids refer to the v3 decision log. Three times, a pre-specified rule could not be applied as written, and we stopped before deciding: the Canoas split rule found no valid split (V48), no noise level in the first scan brought random success below 75\% (V49), and the level rule applied to the extended scan gave inverted levels (V52).
